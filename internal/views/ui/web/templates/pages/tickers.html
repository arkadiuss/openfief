Some institutions, such as XTB, name an instrument only by its ticker and market, with no ISIN. Confirm each ticker's ISIN once: it holds for every account at the same institution. The market-data connector proposes the one it found for the ticker on its market, or type it when it found none. The rows of a ticker and the holdings its statements report wait, left out of the ledger, until its ISIN is confirmed, and are booked then. When the listing names no trading currency and the ticker's exchange quotes in more than one, as London does in pence (GBX), pounds and dollars, pick the one its prices are in: none is assumed.
