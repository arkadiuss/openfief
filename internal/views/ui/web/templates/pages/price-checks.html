A price check compares an institution's own price of an instrument, such as IBKR's mark price, with the latest price its holdings are valued at on or before that day. It is flagged when the two differ by more than {{.Threshold}}, or the threshold set for the instrument. Holdings keep their valuation: look at which price is wrong, such as a wrong listing, a split or pence read as pounds, then acknowledge the check. A pair in two currencies is shown as a currency mismatch: the market-data connector may be pricing another listing than the one held.
