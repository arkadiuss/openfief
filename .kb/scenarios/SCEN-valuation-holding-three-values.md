Given a USD ETF valued at 5 084.09 PLN with open lots costing 4 800.00 PLN at the NBP rate before their purchase, when it is revalued in a taxable account with no fee rule, then net and liquidation are 5 030.11; under a fee rule the sale cost lowers liquidation only; without a rate for a lot's cost or the fee's minimum, without lots, or in IKZE without the owner's marginal rate, the missing value is stored with its reason and the gross value stays.
