Given the demo's application layer applied on kind or k3d, when the install settles, then the Deployment openfief-spicedb is available (its migration ran against the database spicedb of the openfief-db cluster, in the role spicedb that CloudNativePG reconciled); the Job labelled app.kubernetes.io/name=openfief-init-credentials (its name carries the image's short digest, so the test finds it by the label) runs the core's own image, by digest, with init-credentials, and completed, making a preshared key and a role password of 32 bytes or more; the pod is non-root with a read-only root filesystem, no capabilities and no ServiceAccount token, its images pinned by tag and digest, and its key and password read from Secrets; neither SpiceDB's log, the core's nor the Job's holds either value; and a second run of the Job's pod, as a Job of its own name, logs that it kept both Secrets and leaves their values as they were. Runs on both kind and k3d.
