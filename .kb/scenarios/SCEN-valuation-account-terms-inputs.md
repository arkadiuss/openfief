Given two accounts with transactions, when the owner records a wrapper or a fee rule for one, then that account is revalued from its first transaction; the marginal rate revalues both; the number format revalues none; an unknown wrapper, a rate of 100%, a marginal rate of 32 or a format with one separator twice store nothing.
