The owner's account entries must require an account's form, one its wrapper offers, when creating an account in a wrapper with forms, refuse a form for any other wrapper, take a form for an account moved into such a wrapper that has none, and refuse a change to a form once set.

## Context

D-147 has the owner set an OKI account's form when creating it. Letting an account moved into OKI, or created before forms, take one later and never changing a form once set are narrow readings. Modeled by Claude Code for the taxes package D-144..D-150; the author has not reviewed it yet.

## Source

> **[D-147]** The owner sets an OKI account's form when creating it: a bank account, a treasury bond account, a brokerage account or a fund register. The form decides whether its PLN cash is a savings or an investment asset.
