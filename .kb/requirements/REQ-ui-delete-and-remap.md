The built-in UI must offer the owner forms to delete a sleeve, a kind and an account and to remap a mapped broker account, hand each to the core's owner service and show the core's refusal.

## Context

D-120 through the owner services (D-79); the UI leaves every rule to the core. Recorded by the implementation agent of impl/decisions-117; default firmness, not reviewed by the author yet.

## Source

> The owner can delete a sleeve with no accounts, a sleeve kind no sleeve uses and an account with no transactions, and remap a broker account whose OpenFief account has no transactions from it. Nothing the ledger holds is ever deleted (D-18).
