Bonds must count in totals at their net-if-redeemed-today value by default, after the early-redemption fee and Belka tax, with no tax inside IKE.

## Source

> Which bond value counts in totals is configurable; the default is net-if-redeemed-today (after the early-redemption fee and Belka tax, no tax inside IKE).
