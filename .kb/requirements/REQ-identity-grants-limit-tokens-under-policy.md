Under the policy a script token and a view plugin view exactly the accounts their grants cover and change none, as a viewer does, so a token granted one account reads that account and nothing else over the read API and over MCP.

## Context

D-209 limits every holder by grants; D-163's interim rule that other holders of read access view every account ended when the grants became the truth and existing tokens were migrated to explicit household grants. The core's start gives the household grant to the tokens and plugins that held read access, under the policy too.

## Source

> Viewers, script tokens and view plugins are all granted this way, and a grant only ever lets its holder view.
