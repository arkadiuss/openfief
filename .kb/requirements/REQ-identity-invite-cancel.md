The owner can list the viewer invitations still pending and cancel one before it is used; a cancelled link is refused like an expired one, the cancellation is an audit entry, and it needs no passkey check.

## Context

The author answered on 2026-11-13 that pending invitations show on the Access page and that the owner can cancel one before it is used, with a new owner call that is audited and needs no passkey check, since it only takes a way in away. An expired or cancelled link answers the same, revealing nothing about the household.
