Before a question goes to a model the Ask box asks the core once whether it may, with the browser's address, and says why it won't when the day's budget is used up, the session's or the address's limit is reached or the model is busy, in words that give when it ends and that keep the commands working, asks the model nothing then, and falls back to a card that says it couldn't check when the core can't decide.

## Context

D-208's guards end in what the visitor reads; a visitor who is told nothing retries and spends more. The commands answer from the visitor's own record and cost nothing, so they stay.

## Source

> The design session's M5 corrections (2026-11-14): Past a limit the box says so with when it resets and the commands still work; past the budget the Ask box says the day's budget is used up and keeps its commands (M5 brief, 2026-11-14).
