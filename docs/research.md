## Products and prior art

- **[R-1]** (2026-10-25) No existing product combines portfolio tracking with budget-leased, policy-gated agent execution and per-role model routing. The closest open-source pieces:
  - Wealthfolio: AGPL-3.0, Rust/Tauri, local-first, AI assistant and an MCP server. https://github.com/wealthfolio/wealthfolio
  - Ghostfolio: AGPL-3.0, Angular/NestJS/Postgres, multi-user, sells a hosted Premium tier. https://github.com/ghostfolio/ghostfolio
  - TradingAgents: Apache-2.0, LangGraph multi-agent analysis, no execution, supports Ollama and vLLM. https://github.com/TauricResearch/TradingAgents
  - virattt/ai-hedge-fund: MIT, paper trading by default, principle "the LLM never touches the trade". https://github.com/virattt/ai-hedge-fund/blob/main/VISION.md
- **[R-10]** (2026-10-28) Wealthfolio's web edition is one Docker image (`wealthfolio/wealthfolio`, also on GHCR) on port 8088, configured through `WF_*` environment variables, with SQLite on a volume and password or OIDC login; its HTTP endpoints live under `/api/v1`. Activities come in through CSV import in the UI, the paid Wealthfolio Connect broker sync, or its built-in MCP server, whose scoped, revocable tokens grant read access and, optionally, write scopes that draft and then commit activities. The docs describe no public REST API for writing activities.

- **[R-15]** (2026-10-28) Ghostfolio's API, read from its source (main at 9772282 and release 3.93.0, 2026-10-25):
  - Accounts: `POST /api/v1/account` takes `name`, `currency`, `platformId` (may be null), optional `balance`, `comment`, `tags` and a client-chosen `id`, which must be a UUID to be usable in filters. The `isExcluded` flag was removed in 3.39.0; a tag replaces it.
  - Activities: `/api/v1/order` was removed in 3.5.0. `GET /api/v1/activities?accounts=<uuids>` lists them with their `comment`, all at once when `take` is omitted. `DELETE /api/v1/activities/:id` deletes one; `DELETE /api/v1/activities` with filters deletes in bulk, and with no filter deletes everything. Deleting an asset's last activity also deletes the asset and its market prices.
  - Import: `POST /api/v1/import?dryRun=true` validates without writing. The body holds `activities` (comment up to 2000 characters, no client-chosen ID, unknown fields rejected) and optionally `accounts`, `assetProfiles`, `platforms` and `tags`. Duplicates, matched on comment, currency, data source, date, fee, quantity, symbol, type and unit price, are skipped silently. An import isn't rolled back on error. The body is capped at 10 MB.
  - Manual assets: a `MANUAL` symbol must be a UUID (a `GF_` prefix is replaced by a random UUID). Prices are set through `assetProfiles[].marketData` in an import or `POST /api/v1/market-data/MANUAL/:symbol`, by a normal user for their own assets.
  - Comments are blanked in responses when the user's "restricted view" setting is on.
  - Auth: `POST /api/v1/auth/anonymous` exchanges the security token for a bearer token valid for 180 days; rotating the security token doesn't revoke issued ones.
  - Stability: the README's public API covers only auth, health, import, export and public portfolio pages. Accounts, activities and market-data are the web app's internal endpoints, renamed within `/v1` without notice; there is no OpenAPI spec.
  - https://github.com/ghostfolio/ghostfolio (apps/api/src/app/endpoints/import, apps/api/src/app/activities, apps/api/src/services/import/import.service.ts)

- **[R-26]** (2026-11-05) mBank's brokerage, read from its public guides, terms and FAQ and from public sample files and parsers, for structure only. It is called Biuro maklerskie mBanku (mBm, formerly DM mBanku, mDM) and runs two retail services: eMakler inside mBank's online banking (a regular account, plus at most one IKE and one IKZE, each with its own number) and a separate multi-currency platform, mInwestor. Neither offers an API or FIX to retail customers, and PSD2 doesn't reach it: mBank's account information service covers payment accounts, and mBm's own cash accounts settle trades only. The eMakler terms (in force from 2026-06-27) forbid "aplikacje automatyzujące", software that places orders or downloads account operations unless mBm provides it, so only files the owner downloads by hand comply. eMakler exports CSV or PDF for positions (Portfel), orders, trades (Historia transakcji), subscriptions and cash operations (Historia finansowa), back to the account's start. The trade CSV is Windows-1250, `;`-separated, with about 34 `#`-keyed preamble lines that include the holder's name and account numbers, then `Czas transakcji;Papier;Giełda;K/S;Liczba;Kurs;Waluta;Prowizja;Waluta;Wartość;Waluta`. Dates are `DD.MM.YYYY HH:MM:SS`, decimals take a comma and thousands a space. Exchanges are coded like `WWA-GPW`, `USA-NASDAQ`, `GBR-LSE`, `DEU-XETRA`. Older files and the export of today's trades have 9 columns without the fee. No ISIN or ticker appears: `Papier` is a short name, and London rows can be quoted in USD. eMakler settles in PLN, so the price is in the quote currency while the fee and the value are in PLN. The cash CSV is `Data;Opis;Kwota` with the operation's kind only in the free-text description: dividend rows carry the ISIN, the gross rate, the withholding rate and the FX rate, and settlement rows carry the ISIN after `PW:`. A regular eMakler account keeps its cash on the linked bank account (eKonto); IKE and IKZE keep it inside. One PIT-8C covers all accounts. mBank has announced an OKI without a date. https://www.mbank.pl/pdf/inwestycje/emakler/przewodnik_eMakler_3_0.pdf, https://pdf.mbank.pl/mbankpl/of/gielda/emakler/regulamin_emakler_obowiazujacy_od_27.06.2026.pdf, https://www.mbank.pl/indywidualny/inwestycje-i-oszczednosci/gielda/pytania-i-odpowiedzi/rachunek/, https://www.mbank.pl/psd2/slownik-psd2/, https://github.com/pulos-lab/ike-terminal

## Security

- **[R-6]** (2026-10-25) Prompt injection against agents that move money is documented in real incidents, such as the May 2026 Grok/Bankrbot wallet drain. There is research on adversarial news attacks against multi-agent trading systems: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.24069
- **[R-13]** (2026-10-27) Hostname-based egress control for pods. Standard Kubernetes NetworkPolicy matches IP blocks, pods and namespaces, never hostnames. The upstream standard for domain names (`AdminNetworkPolicy` `domainNames`, NPEP-133) is still provisional. Domain policies exist in Cilium (`toFQDNs`), in GKE (`FQDNNetworkPolicy`, which cannot tell apart domains served by the same IP) and in Calico's commercial editions. CNI-independent options: `nais/fqdn-policy`, which writes NetworkPolicy `ipBlock`s from DNS, and Stripe's Smokescreen, a Go CONNECT proxy with per-client allow-lists and a replaceable client-identity function.
  - https://network-policy-api.sigs.k8s.io/npeps/npep-133/
  - https://docs.cloud.google.com/kubernetes-engine/docs/how-to/fqdn-network-policies
  - https://docs.tigera.io/calico-cloud/network-policy/domain-based-policy
  - https://github.com/nais/fqdn-policy
  - https://github.com/stripe/smokescreen

- **[R-14]** (2026-10-28) Smokescreen, read from its source (master at 68e7b82, 2026-10-22):
  - MIT licensed; its vendored dependencies are BSD-3-Clause, MIT and Apache-2.0.
  - Maintained by Stripe: 57 commits in the past year, no open issues. Tags are sparse (v0.1.0 on 2026-09-18, the one before in 2022), and fixes since v0.1.0 include blocking connections to the proxy's own addresses. It is v0, with no API stability promise.
  - Embeddable: package `pkg/smokescreen` exports `NewConfig`, `StartWithConfig` and `BuildProxy`. `Config.RoleFromRequest` receives the CONNECT request with its `Proxy-Authorization` header. `Config.EgressACL` takes a per-role allow-list, which can be built in code; it isn't reloaded at runtime.
  - If `EgressACL` is nil, every destination is allowed. `StartWithConfig` exits the process on errors and handles SIGHUP, SIGTERM and SIGUSR2 itself.
  - It denies loopback, link-local (including cloud metadata addresses), private, CGNAT and IPv4-embedding IPv6 ranges by default, resolves hostnames itself, and logs one decision line per request with role, host and outcome.
  - Go's `net/http` and undici's `ProxyAgent` send `user:password` from the proxy URL as a Basic `Proxy-Authorization` header on CONNECT; Node's built-in client reads `HTTPS_PROXY` only with `NODE_USE_ENV_PROXY=1`.
  - https://github.com/stripe/smokescreen

## Regulation and tax (not legal advice)

- **[R-7]** (2026-10-25) Running agents that trade other people's money is portfolio management, and giving them personalised buy or sell recommendations is investment advice. Both need a KNF permit under art. 69 of the Polish Act on Trading in Financial Instruments. ESMA treats automated client-facing advice as advice. Running your own software on your own account is not an investment service.
  - https://arslege.pl/dzialalnosc-maklerska/k384/a32931/
  - https://www.esma.europa.eu/sites/default/files/2023-07/ESMA35-43-3861_Supervisory_briefing_on_understanding_the_definition_of_advice_under_MiFID_II.pdf
- **[R-8]** (2026-10-25) ESMA's 2024 statement on AI in investment services expects records of decision processes, data sources and algorithms, and disclosure of chatbot use. https://www.esma.europa.eu/sites/default/files/2024-05/ESMA35-335435667-5924__Public_Statement_on_AI_and_investment_services.pdf
- **[R-9]** (2026-10-25) Foreign brokers (IBKR, Trading 212, Revolut) do not issue PIT-8C, so investors compute PIT-38 themselves: 19% Belka tax, FIFO, and the NBP table A exchange rate from the day before. They also file PIT/ZG per country. Open-source references: https://github.com/volodymyr-kovtun/Pitly and https://klopit.co.pl/
- **[R-20]** (2026-10-30) PPK (pracownicze plany kapitałowe), the employee capital plans, from secondary sources rather than the act: the employee pays a basic 2% of gross salary (less for low earners) and may add up to 2% more; the employer pays at least 1.5% and may add up to 2.5%; the state adds a 250 PLN welcome payment and 240 PLN a year. The money sits in target-date funds managed by the employer's chosen institution, so an account's value is units times the fund's published unit price. From 60, taking 25% at once and the rest in at least 120 monthly instalments is free of Belka tax. A withdrawal before 60 (a zwrot) sends 30% of the employer's contributions to the participant's ZUS account, returns the state's payments, and pays 19% on the gains; withdrawals for a first home before 45 or for serious illness follow other rules. The employer's contributions are taxable salary when paid. https://www.analizy.pl/wszystko-o-ppk/40708/wyplata-z-ppk, https://ksiegowosc.infor.pl/podatki/pit/pit/rozliczenia/3072371,Wyplaty-i-zwroty-z-PPK-opodatkowanie-PIT.html, https://www.analizy.pl/wszystko-o-ppk/26954/wplata-powitalna-i-doplaty-roczne-do-ppk-kiedy-i-ile-otrzymuja-uczestnicy-ppk
- **[R-21]** (2026-10-30) PPE (pracownicze programy emerytalne), employer pension programmes: the employer pays a basic contribution, commonly cited as up to 7% of salary, taxed as the employee's income but free of social contributions; the employee may add contributions from taxed salary, up to 42,390 PLN in 2026 under the ministry's announcement of 2025-11-10. A programme runs as a fund, a group life insurance with investment funds, or a pension fund. A payout from 60, or from 55 with early-retirement rights, is free of income and Belka tax. Sources disagree on details, and the 7% ceiling wasn't checked against the act. https://www.gov.pl/web/rodzina/ppe-pracownicze-programy-emerytalne, https://czasopismo.legeartis.org/2025/11/limit-wplat-ikze-2026-oipe-suma-skladek-dodatkowych-ppe-obwieszczenie-mrpips/, https://www.sii.org.pl/16326/edukacja/abc-inwestora/pracownicze-programy-emerytalne-wszystko-co-trzeba-o-nich-wiedziec.html
- **[R-22]** (2026-10-30) Polish tax on restricted stock units (RSU) and employee stock purchase plans (ESPP) is unsettled. Under art. 24 ust. 11 of the PIT act, shares from a qualifying incentive programme, one a joint-stock company (or the employer's parent) creates by a resolution of its general meeting, are taxed only when sold, as capital gains at 19% on PIT-38. Tax-office interpretations disagree on foreign plans: one in July 2025 (0112-KDIL2-2.4011.335.2025.2.WS) refused a foreign company's RSU plan, others in 2025 and March 2026 (0113-KDIPT2-3.4011.32.2026.2.SJ) accepted plans and taxed only the sale, and the NSA has ruled for the taxpayer against the tax office. When a plan doesn't qualify, vesting is taxed as income and the sale's cost is that income. Individual interpretations bind only the one who asked. https://www.inforlex.pl/dok/tresc,FOB0000000000007003880,Interpretacja-indywidualna-z-dnia-11-lipca-2025-r-Dyrektor-Krajowej-Informacji-Skarbowej-sygn-0112-KDIL2-2-4011-335-2025-2-WS.html, https://www.inforlex.pl/dok/tresc,FOB0000000000007551877,Interpretacja-indywidualna-z-dnia-20-marca-2026-r-Dyrektor-Krajowej-Informacji-Skarbowej-sygn-0113-KDIPT2-3-4011-32-2026-2-SJ.html, https://www.partnerinlaw.pl/blog/podatek-od-rsu-programy-motywacyjne-akcje-pracownikom/
- **[R-23]** (2026-10-30) The "czerwona teczka" (red folder), popularized in Polish personal finance by Marcin Iwuć, is a folder of documents and instructions for one's family in case of death: where the will is, which banks, brokers, insurers and pension plans hold what, recurring payments and debts, and who to call. Its guides say never to put passwords, PINs or logins in it, that it doesn't replace a will, and that it needs regular updates. Some money passes outside probate to named people: a bank account's payable-on-death instruction (dyspozycja wkładem na wypadek śmierci, art. 56 of the Banking Law) for the closest family, up to 20 times the average monthly enterprise-sector salary across all such instructions, available on IKE too; IKE, IKZE, PPK and PPE let the holder name beneficiaries. A power of attorney ends with death. https://marciniwuc.com/czerwona-teczka/, https://www.mbank.pl/artykuly/czym-jest-czerwona-teczka/, https://bezprawnik.pl/dyspozycja-wkladem-na-wypadek-smierci-2/, https://www.erste.pl/tfi/edukacja/o-dziedziczeniu-w-inwestowaniu
- **[R-24]** (2026-10-31) XTB's xStation "Historia operacji" export, read from one real export (2025-10-30 to 2026-10-31, an account in PLN holding stocks and ETFs) for its structure only; no value from it is recorded anywhere. The file is named `<account currency>_<account number>_<from>_<to>.xlsx` and holds one account. Its labels are English, and it has three sheets, `Closed Positions`, `Cash Operations` and `Open Positions`:
  - **Rows above the header.** Row 1 is `Account number` and the number as text; row 2 the sheet's title; rows 3 and 4 `Date from (UTC)` and `Date to (UTC)` with datetimes. In `Open Positions`, row 3 is `Data as of report generated`, rows 4 to 6 a summary table (`Product`, `Metric`, `Amount`, `Currency`; metrics `Open position value` and `Open position profit` in the account currency), row 8 a note, and the header is row 9; elsewhere the header is row 5.
  - **`Cash Operations`**, newest first, ending with a `Total` row: `Type`, `Instrument`, `Ticker`, `Category`, `Time`, `Amount`, `ID`, `Comment`, `Product`, `Position ID`. Types seen: `Deposit`, `Withdrawal`, `Stock purchase`, `Stock sell`, `Dividend`, `Withholding tax`, `Free funds interest` and `Free funds interest tax`. `Amount` is in the account currency, negative for money out. `ID` is a 10-digit operation ID as text; `Position ID` links a purchase or sale to its position. Comment formats, here with made-up values: `OPEN BUY 10/10 @ 12.345` and `CLOSE BUY 3/10 @ 12.50` (volume filled, of the order or position, at the instrument's price); `ABC.UK GBP 0.1234/ SHR` (dividend per share, in the instrument's currency); `ABC.UK GBP WHT 15%`; `Free-funds Interest 2026-01` and `Free-funds Interest Tax 2026-01`. A withdrawal's comment names the XTB account number, and a deposit's names the sending bank.
  - **`Closed Positions`**, ending with a totals row: `Instrument`, `Ticker`, `Category` (`STOCK`, `ETF`), `Type` (`BUY` here), `Volume`, `Open Price`, `Open Time (UTC)`, `Close Price`, `Close Time (UTC)`, `Product` (`My Trades`), `Profit/Loss`, `Gross Profit`, `Purchase Value`, `Sale Value`, `Stop Loss`, `Take Profit`, `Commission`, `Margin`, `Swap`, `Rollover`, `Open Conversion Rate`, `Close Conversion Rate`, `Close Origin` (the platform), `Position ID` (a number here, text in `Cash Operations`), `Comment`. - **[R-18]** (2026-10-31) The act on OKI (ustawa z dnia 3 lipca 2026 r. o osobistych kontach inwestycyjnych, Dz.U. 2026 poz. 1098), read from the published text, is in force from 2027-01-01 (art. 46). Income from OKI assets is outside the PIT act (art. 32, adding art. 2 ust. 1 pkt 10 to it), so no Belka tax is due inside OKI, and a loss inside OKI is no tax loss (art. 5 ust. 1 pkt 3 lit. c). A yearly tax on asset value (podatek od wartości aktywów) applies instead. Its base is the sum, over all of a taxpayer's OKI accounts, of each account's average value: the sum of its end-of-day valuations over the tax year plus the payments in, divided by the days the account was held, where a day's payments count only up to that day's withdrawals (art. 23 ust. 1, 2 and 4). Listed securities are valued at market price, treasury savings bonds at nominal plus accrued indexation or capitalization, foreign amounts at the NBP average rate of the last business day before, and a day without a valuation takes the last one (art. 24). Exempt: up to 25,000 PLN of the combined average of savings assets (PLN bank cash, treasury savings bonds, the lowest-risk PRIIP funds, PLN Treasury bills, PLN cash on a bond account) and up to 100,000 PLN of investment assets (qualifying funds, PLN bonds and covered bonds, shares of companies with capital in PLN, PLN cash on a brokerage account), at most 100,000 PLN together, savings assets first; a fund qualifies when at least 70% of its assets are such Polish assets (art. 26 ust. 1, 2 and 4). The limits rise with CPI, rounded down to 100 PLN, from the 2030 tax year (art. 26 ust. 5, art. 44). The rate is 0.85% for 2027 (art. 43), then 19% of the NBP reference rate in force on 31 October of the year before, at least 0.1%, rounded down to two decimal places, announced by 30 November (art. 25). The taxpayer files through e-Urząd Skarbowy from 15 March to 31 May and pays by 31 May; institutions report each account's averages by the end of February (art. 27, 29). Assets may be in PLN or in an EU, EEA or OECD member's currency (art. 14), and the holder may name beneficiaries (art. 9). The act doesn't state how the tax amount is rounded. https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2026/1098/text.pdf
